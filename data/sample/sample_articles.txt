ISLAMABAD - Officials from the World Bank and the Higher Education Commission met in Islamabad on Tuesday to discuss funding for university research. Dr. Ayesha Khan of the University of Karachi said the new programme would support students from Punjab, Sindh and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Reuters reported that the first grants will be announced next month.

GENEVA - The World Health Organization said on Monday that talks with delegations from Brazil, Japan and Germany would resume this week. Prof. Maria Garcia told the BBC that European and Asian partners were close to an agreement. Microsoft and Google also announced new research offices in London.

LAHORE - Heavy rain hit Lahore and Rawalpindi on Friday, the Pakistan Meteorological Department said. Mr. Daniel Carter, a spokesman for Acme Holdings Ltd, said that Pakistani and Chinese engineers were repairing roads near the Indus River. Sarah Johnson said the Urdu report would be published by the United Nations next week.
